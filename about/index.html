About The Breed Book

The Breed Book explains dog breeds one at a time: what they were built for, how they live in a modern home, ten-year cost, and who they fit. Honest, sourced.

Who makes this

The Breed Book is a dog channel on YouTube, and Vision Media publishes it. We research every breed and write the scripts ourselves. The voice is text-to-speech, and each video description mentions it.

How we work

What we say about a breed comes from sources we name: breed clubs, vet schools, published studies on dog behaviour. Each video lists them in its description, and each article lists them at the bottom. Photos are public domain, openly licensed or ours; see the credits page for each video's list.
